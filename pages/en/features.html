folder_open

octopus files

a file manager in the same m3 look, after the macos finder: icons, a list, or columns that open each folder beside the last, with a preview of the file you pick.

  • commitinside a repository: the branch, what changed, what's only local, pull, push and a simple commit. clone from an address or from your github
  • send_to_mobilesend files to a phone with localsend, browse computers on the network, mount google drive with rclone
  • visibilityquick look on space, colored tags, bookmarks, tabs, undo, compress and extract
  • boltsearching every folder below goes through fd, skipping .git, node_modules and target
alarm

reminders, typed

a ! in the spotlight makes one: !10 min tea, !18h bread, !tomorrow at 9 dentist, !alarm 6:45. in english or portuguese.

  • notifications_activea toast on time, to snooze or dismiss; an alarm keeps ringing until you answer
  • repeata small app lists them, edits them and makes them repeat
  • power_settings_newa daemon inside the shell that you can turn off
side_navigation

a real editor for the bar

the settings draw the bar as it will look. tap a module to change it; drag it to move it. the + under the bar opens a menu: add one from the catalog, or make your own.

  • add_circlethe catalog: search, filter by kind, pick the part of the bar, then click a card or drag it in
  • format_painteach module with its own background, m3 shape and options, like the clock's bun or 12 hours
  • boltthe bar changes when you apply, without restarting
construction

modules of your own

a window that builds one, step by step, with the module drawn in a little bar while you fill it in: a name, a material symbol, a command whose output shows under the icon (with a try it button), a command for the click and a look.

view_column

whole bars to start from

default, minimal, monitor, music and focus: each shown as it would look. pick one, then adjust it in the editor.

widgets

widgets you arrange

weather, a clock (analog or digital), your github contributions, the music, a calendar, what's coming up, processor and memory, the battery, a note and a photo, under the windows. they stay put until you ask: right-click the desktop and pick arrange widgets.

  • open_withdrag a widget to move it, its corner to resize it
  • business_centerthe case at the bottom holds the rest: drag one out, or drop one back
  • keyboardesc or done puts them back under the windows
  • visibility_offunder a window, a widget fades out and stops updating until the window goes
tune

each one, your way

the cards' color, their corners and a grid to snap to, for all of them; then each widget's own options under its tune: how many days of weather, a digital clock, which meters, a photo that changes on its own…

calendar_month

your calendar, no login

the dash's calendar and the agenda widget show your events: days with something get a tint, and a click lists them. it reads ical addresses, so a google calendar only needs its secret address: no login, nothing in the cloud console.

  • linkoutlook, proton, nextcloud or a holidays feed work the same
  • cloud_offit keeps a copy, so the events show offline too
palette

your wallpaper, your way

matugen takes the colors from the wallpaper; the settings choose how. nine palette styles, from tonal to fruit salad, a contrast slider, and light, dark or automatic.

the settings themselves are a tree: five big categories, each with its own branches.

lock

a lock that reacts

m3 shapes that turn and change at every key you press, the time, the weather and the line being sung.

partly_cloudy_day

ask the weather
