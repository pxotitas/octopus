some features

the parts of octopus that are nicer to try than to read about. scroll for the play, or pick how you want to see it.

i

ask the weather

partly_cloudy_day

the weather tab takes questions after a >: tomorrow, yesterday, next thursday, last friday, +3, 11/28, the week or the weekend. a week back and two ahead, from open-meteo. try it:

ii

the shapes

every badge, card and button corner in the shell is one of the m3 expressive shapes, drawn as a polar wave so any of them can turn into any other. hover one.

iii

widgets you can move

under the windows live the weather and your github contributions, the little squares from your profile. drag a widget to move it, drag its corner to resize it, and it stays there after a restart. the calendar shows as many weeks as it is wide.
