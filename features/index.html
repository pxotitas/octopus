side_navigation

a real editor for the bar

the settings draw the bar as it will look. drag a module to another spot, tap one to change it, and drag new ones in from the catalog.

  • drag_panfifteen modules: cpu, memory, temperature, brightness, music, volume, keep awake and more
  • format_painteach module with its own background, corners and options, like the clock's bun or 12 hours
  • boltthe bar changes when you apply, without restarting
view_column

whole bars to start from

default, minimal, monitor, music and focus: each shown as it would look. pick one, then adjust it in the editor.

widgets

widgets you arrange

the weather, your github contributions, a clock, the music and a calendar, under the windows. they stay put until you ask: right-click the desktop and pick arrange widgets.

  • open_withdrag a widget to move it, its corner to resize it
  • business_centerthe case at the bottom holds the rest: drag one out, or drop one back
  • keyboardesc or done puts them back under the windows
palette

your wallpaper, your way

matugen takes the colors from the wallpaper; the settings choose how. nine palette styles, from tonal to fruit salad, a contrast slider, and light, dark or automatic.

the settings themselves are a tree: five big categories, each with its own branches.

lock

a lock that reacts

m3 shapes that turn and change at every key you press, the time, the weather and the line being sung.
